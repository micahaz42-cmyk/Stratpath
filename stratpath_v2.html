Chord lab

A chord is several notes played together. The diagram below shows which strings to press. String 6 is the thickest (low E), string 1 is the thinnest (high e).

Numbers inside dots are suggested finger numbers: 1=index, 2=middle, 3=ring, 4=pinky. O=open string, X=don't play.

How to test: strum slowly from the lowest allowed string. Each note should ring without buzzing. If one string is muted, adjust finger angle and press just behind the fret.

Chord-change challenge

Set a 60-second timer. Switch between two chords as many times as you can while keeping each one clear. Write your result and try again tomorrow.

From thickest to thinnest: E – A – D – G – B – e. Press a string name to hear its approximate reference pitch.

Tune with the LT25's built-in tuner or a dedicated tuner for accuracy. These sounds are references, not a microphone-based tuner.

Guitar basics, explained

Parts of your guitar

Headstock: the top where the tuning pegs are. Nut: the small guide at the top of the neck. Fret: a metal strip across the neck. Pickup: converts string vibration into an electrical signal. Bridge: holds the strings at the body.

How to hold it

Sit upright, guitar body resting on your leg, neck pointing slightly upward. Keep your wrist reasonably relaxed. Place your thumb behind the neck. Don't squeeze harder than necessary.

How to hold a pick

Place a pick on the side of your curled index finger and rest your thumb on top. Let only a small tip stick out. Use short, relaxed strokes across the strings.

How to fret a note

Press a string with the fingertip just behind a metal fret (toward the headstock), not directly on the metal. Pick once. If it buzzes, reposition and use slightly more pressure.

How to read TAB

Six horizontal lines represent strings. The bottom line is the thickest string. Numbers show which fret to press. A 0 means play an open string. Read from left to right.

Terms you'll hear

Chord: notes played together. Riff: a recognizable short musical phrase. Scale: notes arranged in a pattern. BPM: beats per minute. Downstroke: pick toward the floor. Upstroke: pick toward the ceiling. Barre: one finger holding several strings.

HSS pickup layout

Your Strat has a humbucker near the bridge and two single-coil pickups. The humbucker often sounds fuller and handles distortion well; the single-coils often sound brighter or glassier.

Getting sound from the LT25

Plug a guitar cable into the guitar and amp input, turn guitar volume up, select a clean preset, then raise the amp's master volume gradually. If silent, check the cable, guitar volume, and amp preset.
